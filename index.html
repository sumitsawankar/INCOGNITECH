About Me

Sumit Sawankar - Student Developer & Innovator

Hi! I'm Sumit Sawankar, a passionate student developer who believes in the power of collaboration and knowledge sharing. INCOGNITECH was born from my own experiences struggling to find study materials, project partners, and opportunities to connect with like-minded students.

As an Information Technology student pursuing B.Tech, I've experienced firsthand the challenges of academic life - from finding quality study notes to connecting with peers for group projects. That's why I created this platform to bridge these gaps and empower students worldwide.
